Longevity Essentials for 50+
  • Dietary Fiber — Aim for 25–35 g/day from oats, lentils, and leafy greens.
  • Calcium — 1,000–1,200 mg/day via dairy, tofu, sesame seeds, or fortified foods.
  • Vitamin D3 — 800–1,000 IU/day. Enables calcium absorption and supports immune function.
  • Omega-3 Fatty Acids — 2–3 servings of fatty fish weekly (or 1 g EPA/DHA supplement).
  • Antioxidant Foods — Berries, turmeric, dark leafy greens, and walnuts to neutralize oxidative stress.

Joint Health Rules
  • Always warm up — 5–10 min of light movement before any workout.
  • Pain ≠ progress — Stop on sharp joint pain. Muscle burn is normal; joint pain is a warning.
  • Rest days required — 1–2 full rest days per week.
  • Balance training — Heel-to-toe walks and single-leg stands reduce fall risk.
